WELCOME TO THE NETWORK
The PTR Alumni Network is the official digital community of the Primary Teacher
Residency Program. It exists to connect graduates, share classroom practice, and
document the impact of investing in teachers.

A MESSAGE TO THE FIRST COHORT
On 5 August 2024, 100 Primary Teacher Residents completed the pilot programme.
That milestone belongs to every resident, tutor, mentor, school leader, and
partner who made the residency year possible. You are now part of a network that
can support one another for years to come.

WHAT THE PILOT BUILT
- A 12-month residency model combining classroom instruction and guided practice.
- 100 trained resident teachers.
- Two host Teacher Training Colleges: TTC Save and TTC de la Salle Byumba.
- 17 primary demonstration schools.
- A partnership of government, universities, and education organisations.

FROM RESIDENT TO ALUMNUS
The end of the residency is not the end of learning. The network is here to help
you:
  - stay connected with fellow graduates,
  - share lesson ideas and resources,
  - find peer support when a classroom challenge arises,
  - take part in professional learning and alumni events,
  - tell the story of what your learners are achieving.

HOW TO GET INVOLVED
1. Complete a member profile through the Join & Contact page.
2. Share a lesson idea, photo, or story for review.
3. Join the next alumni gathering.
4. Encourage another PTRP graduate to sign up.
5. Use the resource library and adapt the tools to your classroom.

A PRACTICE TO TRY THIS WEEK
Plan one lesson around a single strong question instead of a list of facts.
Give learners time to think, discuss with a partner, and explain their reasoning.
Ask: "What makes you say that?" Then listen. A small change in questioning can
change the whole lesson.
